Microneedling with Exo Booster

Next-generation microneedling – for visibly revitalised skin.

This innovative treatment combines classic microneedling with an advanced Exo Booster based on Lactobacillus exosomes.

The formula, with 10 billion exosomes per treatment, is complemented by valuable omega-3 fatty acids, Bacillus ferment, peptides and phospholipids from olive stem cells.

Together, they support skin regeneration, strengthen the skin barrier and promote skin elasticity as well as a smoother, more even complexion.

The result: a revitalised, fresh and well-cared-for complexion with refined skin texture and a new radiance.

  • Supports cell renewal
  • Strengthens the skin barrier
  • Promotes elasticity and firmness
